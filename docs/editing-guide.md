# Editing the Transition VC website

Everything on the website — team, portfolio, news, events, reports, fund figures and page
text — is edited in the admin at **`/admin`** (for example
`https://www.transitionventurecapital.com/admin`). No code is needed. Changes appear on the live
site within a few seconds of saving.

Substack essays and YouTube videos appear **automatically** within an hour of being published —
you don't need to add them here.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to `/admin` and sign in with your email and password.
2. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen and follow the email.
3. New editors are invited by an **Admin** (Settings → Users → Create new).

**Roles**
- **Editor** — can change all website content.
- **Admin** — can also change fund figures, funds and SEBI details, read website enquiries
  (pitch decks) and manage users.

## Common tasks

### Add a new portfolio company
1. **Content → Portfolio → Create new**.
2. **Basics** tab: name, one-line summary, sector, stage, year invested, website, and logo
   (SVG or transparent PNG is best).
3. **Profile** tab (optional): founders, "Why we invested", the Substack essay about the company
   (paste its link — the essay and company are then linked automatically), and a video link.
4. Click **Publish**. It appears on the Portfolio page and the map.
   - Use **Save draft** instead to prepare it without it going live.
   - Tick **Feature on home page** (right-hand side) to show it in the home page's featured
     portfolio section — add a **Cover image** (Basics tab) too. 3–6 featured companies works best.

### Mark a company as exited or acquired
Open the company → **Status** tab → choose *Exited* or *Acquired*, add the year and a short note
(e.g. "Acquired by …") → **Publish**. It gets a badge and is no longer counted as an active company.

### Add or update a team member
**Content → Team → Create new** (or open an existing person). Choose their section (Founding
Team, Investments, Investor Relations, Capital Formation, Portfolio Investments, Marketing &
Engagements, Finance, Human Resources, or Sector Experts & Advisors), add a portrait photo, job
title and LinkedIn link, then **Publish**. To remove someone without deleting them, untick
**Show on website**.

*Photos:* waist-up, 4:5 (ideally 1200 × 1500 px), on the same light studio background as the
others, so the grid looks even. The Founding Team appears large at the top of the team section,
with their **Short bio**; everyone else appears in a grid under their section.

*Team photo:* the group photo above the team is under **Pages → About page**.

*Order:* each entry has a **Display order** number — lower numbers appear first. Use gaps
(10, 20, 30…) so you can slot people in between later.

### Add a news article
**Content → News → Create new**: headline, publication, date and link → **Save**. The newest
items show first on Perspectives and the home page.

### Add an event or a report
- **Content → Events**: name, dates, location, description, image and registration link.
  Upcoming events appear first; the next one is also shown on the home page.
- **Content → Reports & publications**: title, date, summary, cover image and either a PDF upload
  or an external link.

### Edit the home page
**Pages → Home page** has three tabs:
- **Positioning** — the line shown above the headline figures.
- **Why founders choose us** — 4–5 short pillars, each with a title, one sentence and an icon.
- **Founder FAQ** — questions and answers for founders (cheque size, stages, how to pitch…).
  Good additions once agreed internally: how long a decision usually takes, and what to include
  in a pitch deck.

The headline figures come from **Fund metrics** (Admins tick *Show on home page* on 3–4 of them).
The "Where we invest" sectors are under **Pages → Home: where we invest**.
Those sectors also drive the top of the home page: the last word of the headline cycles through
the sector names, and the card beside it shows a company from that sector (tick *Feature on home
page* on a company to make it the one shown; otherwise the first in that sector is used). For a
company to appear, link its sector under *Portfolio sector* on the "Where we invest" entry.

### Change fund figures, funds or SEBI details *(Admins only)*
- **Fund information → Fund metrics**: the track-record figures and their "as of" date. Tick
  *Show on home page* on the 3–4 figures to feature on the home page; all of them appear on Funds.
- **Fund information → Funds**: each fund's size, status, description and SEBI registration
  (shown in the site footer).

These are regulated disclosures — please have them checked before publishing. Every change is
recorded in the version history.

### Show an announcement banner
**Settings → Announcement bar**: tick **Show the banner**, write the message (and an optional
link), and optionally set start/end dates. It appears at the top of every page.

### Update page text, contact details or links
- **Settings → Site settings**: taglines, About text, investment criteria, contact details,
  social links, Investor Login link, policy PDFs.
- **Pages**: the Impact page, the GIFT City page, and the home page's "Where we invest" sectors.
- **Settings → Search & sharing (SEO)**: how each page appears in Google and when shared.

### Read website enquiries *(Admins only)*
**Inbox → Enquiries** lists everything sent through the website forms. Open one to read it and
download any pitch deck. Set **Status** to *Reviewed* or *Archived* to keep the inbox tidy.

---

## Undoing a change
Open the item → **Versions** (top of the page) → pick an earlier version → **Restore**.

## Images
Upload images when editing, or in **Library → Images & files**. Always fill in the short
description — it's read aloud to visitors using screen readers. Photos: at least 1200 × 1500 px.
Logos: SVG or a transparent PNG.

## Need a new sector, page or layout change?
Sectors, page layouts, colours and animations are managed by the developer so the site stays
consistent. Ask them for these changes.
